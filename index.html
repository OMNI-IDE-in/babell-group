What is India's first DROC software? +

Omni Cero DROC is India's first DROC software and first multi-brand Digital Radiography Operating Console, developed by Omniide in Bengaluru, India. It natively connects flat-panel detectors from 11 global manufacturers (including Varex Imaging, iRay Technology, Vieworks, DRTECH, Rayence, Canon Medical, CareRay, Carestream, Thales Group, Teledyne DALSA, and PIXXGEN), high-frequency generators, and DICOM dry laser film imagers in a 100% offline Windows workstation environment. Explore the official platform at cero.omniide.com ↗.

What is India's first multi-brand DROC software and why is it significant? +

Historically, Indian diagnostic centers, hospitals, and imaging equipment manufacturers were locked into proprietary single-brand consoles with exorbitant licensing costs and forced hardware obsolescence. Omni Cero is India's first multi-brand DROC software, offering universal plug-and-play compatibility across 11 global detector brands, integrated generator exposure control, sub-second exposure preview with Automatic Exposure Detection (AED), and full local PACS archiving with zero cloud dependencies.

Which flat-panel detector brands are supported by Omni Cero DROC? +

Omni Cero DROC natively supports 11 leading flat-panel detector manufacturers: Varex Imaging (PaxScan), iRay Technology, Vieworks (VIVIX), DRTECH, Rayence, CareRay, Teledyne DALSA, Canon Medical (CXDI), Carestream (DRX), Thales Group, and PIXXGEN Corporation. It also connects with high-frequency generators (CPI, Sedecal, Gulmay, Poskom) and DICOM dry laser printers (Fuji, Agfa, Carestream, Sony).

Where can hospitals and diagnostic centers evaluate or download Omni Cero DROC? +

Clinical teams, radiologists, hospital procurement leads, and equipment manufacturers can review technical specifications, hardware compatibility tables, and download production workstation installers directly on the official platform at https://cero.omniide.com ↗ or contact our Bengaluru engineering lab.

What does the complete Cero product family include? +

The Cero product family spans three interconnected medical imaging solutions: Omni Cero DROC (India's first multi-brand digital radiography acquisition), Live Cero (real-time 30 FPS fluoroscopy capture and Digital Subtraction Angiography for surgical C-arms), and Cero XVR (tracker-free 3D surgical navigation with sub-millimeter accuracy aligning 2D X-rays with CT/MRI volumes).

Is Omniide only an AI developer-tools company? +

No. Omniide is an independent research and engineering company working across agentic AI, software engineering, and healthcare technology. OmniIDE serves developer workflows, while Omni Cero Medical Systems is our flagship medtech family spanning digital radiography, fluoroscopy, and navigation.

Is Omniide an accredited entity with the Government of India? +

Yes. Omniide is officially registered as an MSME with the Government of India (#UDYAM-KR-03-0681404), recognized under the DPIIT Startup India program, listed on the Bhaskar Registry (IN-0526-9460PZ), and verified on NSWS.
