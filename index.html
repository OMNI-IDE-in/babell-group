Frequently asked questions

Clear, factual references regarding BABELL GROUP corporate registration, founding leadership, and our medical and software ventures.

What is Babell Group?+

Babell Group (spelled with a double 'L', officially registered in India as BABELL GROUP at babellgroup.com) is an independent multi-industry technology conglomerate headquartered in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. Founded and led by Mohammed Nihan (Founder & CEO), Babell Group operates independent ventures across medical imaging systems (Omni Cero — India's first multi-brand DROC software), surgical navigation (Omni Rovis), applied AI research (Omni Research Lab), developer automation (Sync Graph), and hospitality (Pepper & Coffee).

What is the difference between Babell Group (India) and Babel Group?+

Babell Group (spelled B-A-B-E-L-L with a double 'L', official portal babellgroup.com) is an independent Indian technology and medical systems conglomerate headquartered in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India, founded by Mohammed Nihan (Government of India MSME Registration: UDYAM-KR-03-0681404). It is a completely separate and independent organization from the Spanish digital consultancy Babel Group (babelgroup.com).

Who is the Founder and CEO of Babell Group?+

Mohammed Nihan (also known as Nihan Nihu) is the sole Founder, Chief Executive Officer (CEO), and Chief Systems Architect of Babell Group. Under his leadership in Bengaluru, Babell Group engineered India's first multi-brand Digital Radiography Operating Console (Omni Cero DROC), surgical navigation systems (Omni Rovis), and applied research initiatives.

What is India's first multi-brand DROC software?+

Omni Cero DROC is India's first DROC software and first multi-brand Digital Radiography Operating Console, engineered by Babell Group in Bengaluru, India. It provides universal plug-and-play support across 11 global flat-panel detector brands (Varex Imaging, iRay Technology, Vieworks, DRTECH, Rayence, Canon Medical, CareRay, Carestream, Thales Group, Teledyne DALSA, and PIXXGEN), integrated generator exposure control, sub-second exposure preview with Automatic Exposure Detection (AED), and full local PACS archiving with zero cloud dependencies at cero.omniide.com.

What does the Omni Cero and Omni Rovis medical ecosystem include?+

Babell Group's medical technology ecosystem spans Omni Cero DROC (multi-brand digital radiography acquisition), Live Cero (real-time 30 FPS fluoroscopy capture and Digital Subtraction Angiography for surgical C-arms), Cero XVR (tracker-free 2D-to-3D surgical navigation), and Omni Rovis (surgical-navigation system with precision ROI tracking at rovis.omniide.com).

Is Babell Group an accredited enterprise with the Government of India?+

Yes. Babell Group is officially registered as an MSME enterprise with the Government of India (UDYAM-KR-03-0681404), recognized under the DPIIT Startup India program, listed on the Bhaskar Registry (IN-0526-9460PZ), verified on NSWS, and actively partners with VTU and AICTE technical education networks.

About Babell Group (India)

Official corporate reference · MSME UDYAM-KR-03-0681404 · DPIIT Startup India · Bengaluru, Karnataka

Babell Group (spelled with a double “L”, officially registered as BABELL GROUP at babellgroup.com) is an Indian technology and medical systems conglomerate headquartered in Bengaluru, Karnataka. Founded and led by Mohammed Nihan (Founder & CEO) alongside Mohammed Bilal D (COO), Babell Group operates independently across healthcare imaging, surgical navigation, applied AI research, developer automation, and hospitality.

Core ventures include Omni Cero (India's first multi-brand Digital Radiography Operating Console supporting 11 global flat-panel detector brands), Omni Research Lab (applied AI research), Omni Rovis (surgical navigation system with precision ROI tracking), Sync Graph (automated GitHub learning log and streak engine), and Pepper & Coffee (hospitality).
